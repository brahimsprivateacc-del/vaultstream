Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

VaultStream ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website vaultstream.online. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use VaultStream:

  • Account information — your email address, username, and password when you sign up
  • Profile information — your profile photo and bio if you choose to add them
  • Content — videos you upload to the platform
  • Usage data — videos you watch, likes, and comments you leave
  • Device information — browser type, device type, and IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the VaultStream platform
  • Allow you to upload, share and watch videos
  • Display your profile to other users
  • Show view counts, likes and comments
  • Improve the platform and fix bugs
  • Show advertisements to support the platform

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate VaultStream:

  • Supabase — stores your account data and video information
  • Cloudinary — stores and delivers your uploaded videos and images
  • Render — hosts the VaultStream website
  • Google AdSense — shows advertisements on the platform

Each of these services has their own privacy policy. We recommend reading them if you have concerns.

4. Cookies

VaultStream uses cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences such as dark/light mode. We also use cookies for advertising purposes through Google AdSense.

5. Your Videos

Videos you upload to VaultStream are stored on Cloudinary and are publicly accessible to all users of the platform. Do not upload videos containing private or sensitive information.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and data by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

VaultStream is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to processing of your data

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@vaultstream.online
