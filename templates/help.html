🚀 Getting Started
1
Create your account
Click Sign Up in the top right corner. Enter a username, your email address and a password (at least 6 characters). Then click Create Account!
💡 Tip: Choose a cool username — it will show on all your videos!
2
Sign in to your account
Already have an account? Click Sign In in the top right. Enter your email and password, then click Sign in. You'll stay logged in automatically!
💡 Tip: You don't need to sign in every time — we remember you for a whole year!
3
Set up your profile
Click your username in the top right → click your avatar to upload a profile photo → click Change photo. Add a bio to tell people about yourself!
💡 Tip: A profile photo makes your channel look way more professional!
4
Upload your first video
Click the 📤 Upload button in the top right. Give your video a title, pick a category, then drag and drop your video file or click Select File. Supports MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI and WEBM!
💡 Tip: For Shorts (vertical videos) check the 📱 "Upload as Short" box before uploading!
5
Watch and interact
Click any video to watch it. You can ❤️ like videos, 💬 leave comments, and 💾 save videos offline to watch without internet!
💡 Tip: Use the search bar at the top to find specific videos!
6
Go Live!
Click 🔴 Go Live in the sidebar or top bar. Enter a stream title and click Start Streaming. Copy the link and share it with friends so they can watch live!
💡 Tip: You can share your screen by clicking the 🖥️ Share Screen button while live!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I reset my password? ▼
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password" — you'll receive an email with a reset link. Make sure to check your spam folder!
What video formats are supported? ▼
VaultStream supports MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI and WEBM video formats. MP4 is recommended for best compatibility. Maximum file size is 4GB.
How do I install VaultStream as an app on my phone? ▼
On iPhone: Open vaultstream.online in Safari → tap the Share button → tap "Add to Home Screen". On Android: Open in Chrome → tap the menu → tap "Add to Home Screen". It works just like a real app!
Why does my video not show up after uploading? ▼
Videos are uploaded to the cloud and saved to the database automatically. Try refreshing the page. If it still doesn't appear, make sure you were logged in when you uploaded.
Can I watch videos without internet? ▼
Yes! Open any video and click the "💾 Save Offline" button. The video will download to your device and you can watch it anytime without internet.
How do I delete one of my videos? ▼
Open the video you want to delete and click the red "🗑 Delete" button on the right side. Only you can delete your own videos.
How do I report a video? ▼
Click the ⋮ menu on any video card on the home page and select "🚩 Report". Choose a reason and submit — our admin will review it.
